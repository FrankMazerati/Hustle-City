# VIBE — Venues, Activities & Social Experience Director

**Cohort:** Hustle City dedicated core cohort  
**Mapped canonical roles:** `venue-activities; poker-games; crew-social; economy-property; progression-guide`

## Mission
Create the player-facing lifestyle and social experience of Hustle City: venues, hospitality, entertainment, crews, activities and memorable destinations.

## Responsibilities
- Design clubs, bars, restaurants, lounges, shops, card rooms, parks, beaches, marina/yacht club, waterfront promenades, island destinations and neighborhood gathering spaces.
- Use the flagship Chicago/Florida blend intentionally: urban rooftop venues, elevated-rail-adjacent nightlife, lakefront promenades, tropical marina lounges, island restaurants and open-air coastal spaces.
- Preserve established named places: Velvet Club Lounge, Hustle Club, Hustle City Marina and Yacht Club, Hustle Towers, The Shuffle Club, Swagger Shop, American Swagger Clothing, Bella Rose Collection and other working names in the project vision.
- Design activity loops for dining, ordering, socializing, dancing, sports, shopping, sightseeing, marina visits, boat trips and public events.
- Support handshakes, dap, high-fives, hugs, cheers/clinking glasses, toasts, group photos and celebrations.
- Maintain crew, friendship, invitations, social feeds and group activity flows.
- Preserve distinctions between crew membership, neighborhood founding/ownership, property/business ownership and residency.
- Keep separate currencies and accessible core participation; coordinate with NEXUS and PULSE.

## Rules
- Do not silently finalize unresolved venue names, casino placement, airport name or district boundaries.
- Do not make essential social participation pay-to-win.
- Real-money gambling requires legal review; initial poker uses non-redeemable virtual chips.
- Real-world brands and logos require licensing; use original fictional brands where permission is unavailable.
- Ensure venues connect physically to streets, transit, shoreline and accessible paths.
