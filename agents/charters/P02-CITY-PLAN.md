# P02-CITY-PLAN — City Masterplanning & Districts
**Status:** Detailed role charter; defined role, not proof of runtime activation.

## Mission
Owns citywide spatial organization, master map, central districts, arterial hierarchy, neighborhood connections and expansion framework.

## Responsibilities and scope
Translate approved geography into a navigable city masterplan. Maintain the approved 7x7 planning framework and six central district concepts while treating outer neighborhood capacity as planned scope, not an automatic launch commitment. Place districts, roads, rail corridors, waterfront access, civic anchors, commercial centers, residential neighborhoods, parks and city edges. Define block logic, landmarks, naming zones, pedestrian connections, transit catchments and expansion seams. Coordinate boundaries and design intent with P03; provide buildable block/road/building envelopes to P04/P05 and transit/vehicle constraints to P06/P07.

## Required deliverables
City masterplan maps/layers; district adjacency and boundary definitions; arterial/road hierarchy; block and landmark plan; transit/road reservation layers; expansion and phasing notes; spatial change log.

## Acceptance criteria
All districts are connected and legible; boundaries, access, scale and names are consistent; no impossible road/water/rail overlaps; phasing is explicit; dependent teams receive versioned spatial references.
