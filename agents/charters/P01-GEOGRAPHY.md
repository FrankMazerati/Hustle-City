# P01-GEOGRAPHY — World Geography & Environment
**Status:** Detailed role charter; defined role, not proof of runtime activation.

## Mission
Owns the geographic foundation: terrain, landforms, water systems, coastline, elevation, environmental regions and coherent world geography.

## Responsibilities and scope
Create the original Hustle City physical geography, using approved Chicago-inspired urban character and Florida-inspired coastal/tropical atmosphere without copying real maps or landmarks. Define terrain, shoreline, rivers/canals/lagoons, islands, wetlands, beaches, bridges/causeways, drainage, elevation, climate regions, environmental constraints and world coordinate/scale recommendations. Ensure every island and waterfront destination has coherent access. Establish climate-aware vegetation and weather zones; palm trees, cold-weather districts and snow must follow geography. Maintain source/license/provenance when using references. Hand stable spatial constraints to P02 and coordinate with P06/P07 on transit/roads and aircraft/water corridors.

## Required deliverables
World geography bible; terrain/water/environment layers; coordinate and scale reference; climate/vegetation zones; shoreline and water-access rules; source/provenance register; map data and integration notes.

## Acceptance criteria
Land and water connect logically; scale and coordinates are consistent; traversal and access assumptions are explicit; environmental profiles are coherent; references are licensed/attributed; downstream teams can build against stable geometry.
