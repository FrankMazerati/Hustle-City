# Hustle City — Top-Down District and Landmark Plan

**Status:** Creator-directed planning baseline; exact geography and coordinates remain unresolved.  
**Branch:** `knowledge-library-foundation`  
**Purpose:** Give the World Architect an actionable visual-planning brief and establish a spatial baseline for the future City Planner.

## 1. Creator-approved spatial composition

### Diamond District — central downtown
- Anchor the city plan with the Diamond District as the central downtown/high-rise core.
- Place Diamond Row in the downtown/Diamond District context as a distinct destination or commercial strip.
- Downtown should have a recognizable skyline and dense, connected streets.

### Downtown nightlife and cigar venues
- Place the Velvet Lounge, cigar bar/cigar lounges and a small selection of additional lounges in the downtown/Diamond District area.
- A few of these venues should be fully functional on day one.
- Exact additional venue identities and count are unresolved; do not invent canon names.

### Swagger District — fashion and clothing
- Establish Swagger District as the fashion/clothing district.
- Include clothing shops, shoe stores and related style retail.
- Give the district a distinct visual character while preserving the established Swagger identity.

### Waterfront — automotive, aviation and yacht access
- Group the car lot, airport and yacht club in the waterfront-oriented part of the master plan.
- Make the shoreline/waterfront legible through coherent geography, access roads and transport links.
- The exact adjacency and parcel configuration must account for runway safety/airport land needs as well as marina, yacht-club and shoreline access. The creator's requested grouping is approved; detailed land-use geometry is not yet fixed.

### Entertainment/media campus — blue and black
Place these three creator-named entities together in one shared campus/area:
1. Upper Level Entertainment Studios
2. Upper Level Films
3. Monster Beats

Their shared visual identity is **blue and black**. They remain three distinct entities/buildings or operations within a coordinated campus identity. Do not scatter them across unrelated districts.

### Residential coverage
- The initial playable release must contain a selection of playable houses in every neighborhood.
- This requirement is about coverage across every neighborhood, not a currently fixed house count.
- Exact properties and number per neighborhood remain unresolved.

## 3. Existing world-planning constraints to preserve

- Master planning framework: 7x7 = 49 large planning boxes.
- Six central district concepts and 43 potential outer neighborhood boxes.
- Each outer neighborhood box can be planned as 5x5 city blocks.
- This is long-term world capacity, not a requirement that every box be fully built for the first release.
- Hustle District remains upscale in appearance/clientele; Trapper District remains distressed, gritty and lower-income in contrast.
- Hustle City retains its original fictional geography, blending selected Chicago-inspired urban character with Florida-inspired tropical/coastal atmosphere without copying either real city.
- The two established train stations and airport remain required playable launch transportation destinations; the airport is also part of the creator-directed waterfront grouping.

## 4. World Architect production brief

Build the first top-down visual master plan in layers:

1. **Macro geography:** coastline/waterfront shape, beach/water access, terrain and skyline silhouette.
2. **District framework:** central Diamond downtown; Swagger fashion district; the other four established district concepts shown without invented boundaries.
3. **Signature clusters:** downtown nightlife/cigar venues; waterfront car lot/airport/yacht club; co-located blue/black media campus.
4. **Mobility skeleton:** primary roads, arterial connections, rail corridors, both train stations, airport access, pedestrian and waterfront connections.
5. **Residential coverage:** indicate a playable residential selection in every neighborhood.
6. **Landmark and identity pass:** skyline hierarchy, district signage, campus branding, waterfront identity, lighting and architectural families.

The first output should be a readable overhead planning image/board with a legend, clear labels, north arrow only once orientation is approved, and an explicit “conceptual / not to scale” note until geometry is finalized.

## 5. City Planner handoff

After the World Architect establishes the visual composition, the City Planner should resolve:
- Exact compass orientation and district boundaries.
- Road hierarchy, intersections, bridges, pedestrian links and transit corridors.
- Block dimensions, block IDs, parcel boundaries and building footprints.
- Airport runway/terminal/approach geometry and safe separation from dense development.
- Marina, yacht-club, car-lot and waterfront access geometry.
- Media-campus parcels and internal circulation.
- Diamond Row limits and the downtown venue parcel plan.
- The two train stations' locations, entrances, routes and service coverage.
- Playable house selections and residential access in every neighborhood.

## 6. Explicitly unresolved — do not invent

- Exact position/footprint of the media campus and waterfront parcels.
- Whether airport and yacht club are directly adjacent or within a connected waterfront zone.
- Final Diamond Row boundaries.
- Names and count of additional downtown lounges.
- Exact playable house count and property IDs in each neighborhood.
- Exact road, rail, flight and water routes.
