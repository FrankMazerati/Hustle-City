# Geographic Research and Mapping Policy

Use OpenStreetMap and suitable public geospatial datasets for roads, building footprints, paths, terrain, waterways, bridges and transit context. Review attribution, license and share-alike requirements, including ODbL where relevant. CTA open data may inform routes, stops, schedules and service behavior where data and terms permit; verify dataset freshness, coverage and license.

Google Maps/Earth may be used only as permitted visual/geographic reference. Do not scrape, trace, export or incorporate imagery/geometry into shipped assets contrary to terms or without required permission.

Each atlas record must include area ID, source, source date, license, attribution, coordinates/CRS, geometry type, confidence, feature category, proposed fictional adaptation, review status and Godot implementation notes.

Hustle City is not a 1:1 replica. Use references to inform recognizable urban logic and atmosphere while creating original city names, brands, layouts and creative expression.
