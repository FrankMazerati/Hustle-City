# Hustle City — Fashion Hub and Hustle Towers Foundation

**Status:** Creator-directed foundation; detailed plans remain subject to creator review.  
**Primary engine:** Unreal Engine 5. **Browser companion:** Babylon.js preview.  
**Location:** Fashion Hub / Fashion–Swagger District working label; exact district boundaries remain open.

## 1. Creator direction captured

The Fashion Hub is to include a major vertical landmark called **Hustle Towers**, specified by the creator as **100 floors**. The composition is to rise **straight up**: prioritize a vertical tower and vertical circulation rather than spreading the landmark into a sprawling horizontal campus.

The creator also requested the following experiences and destinations:
- Malls / shopping areas.
- Multiple casino areas.
- Multiple food concierge lounges.
- Multiple welcoming help desks / welcome desks.
- The **Heavenly Hallway**.
- “Kicking areas” — creator wording retained verbatim; exact intended activity and design are not yet confirmed.
- **Caesar's Palace** — named by the creator in this context; its exact relationship to Hustle Towers (venue inside the tower, attached vertical component, or separate destination) remains unresolved.

These are recorded as requested program elements, not as finished floor assignments or completed assets.

## 2. Vertical-only massing rule

- Hustle Towers is a 100-floor vertical landmark.
- The principal silhouette must be tall and vertically composed.
- Do not turn the concept into a sprawling resort campus, broad low-rise mall, or horizontally spread casino complex.
- Retail, entertainment, hospitality, food, concierge, and casino functions should be organized vertically and connected by clear internal circulation.
- Ground-level entrances and necessary service/loading access are allowed; they must not undermine the vertical-first identity.
- Any podium, connecting wing, or separate Caesar's Palace structure requires creator approval before it becomes canonical.

## 3. Program requirements — no floor allocation is locked yet

The following functions must be accommodated within the vertical plan:
1. **Mall spaces:** one or more shopping environments, with vertical circulation and legible retail wayfinding.
2. **Casino spaces:** multiple distinct casino areas; their exact number, themes, operating rules, and floor positions remain open.
3. **Food concierge lounges:** multiple lounges, with food discovery, ordering/assistance, waiting, seating, and guest support.
4. **Welcoming help desks:** multiple guest-assistance points distributed where visitors need orientation and service. They should not be represented by one lonely desk for the entire 100-floor building.
5. **Heavenly Hallway:** a named signature interior experience. Its visual style, exact location, length, and gameplay function await creator direction.
6. **Kicking areas:** preserve the requested name. A possible interpretation is relaxed social/hangout space, but do not finalize that interpretation without confirmation.
7. **Caesar's Palace:** retain the exact creator-provided name in planning records. Its relationship to Hustle Towers is unresolved.

Do not assign exact floor numbers to these uses until a floor-by-floor brief is reviewed. Keep a 100-floor register so every floor can eventually receive a unique ID, purpose, access rules, services, and content status.

## 4. Vertical circulation and building logic

The 100-floor building must be designed as a believable high-rise:
- Multiple passenger elevator groups sized and zoned for lower, middle, and upper floor destinations.
- Service elevators separated from primary guest circulation where practical.
- Protected stair/e-stair cores, emergency egress, fire/life-safety systems, refuge and service areas as required by the eventual engineering design.
- Clear arrival, transfer, and wayfinding logic so guests can find malls, casinos, lounges, help desks, and the Heavenly Hallway.
- Accessible routes and redundant circulation paths.
- Structural core, mechanical/electrical/plumbing risers, plant rooms, loading/service access, and realistic floor-to-floor heights.
- No impossible unsupported cantilevers or decorative geometry that conceals an incoherent structure.

Exact dimensions, elevator counts, structural system, floor heights, and building code assumptions must be established during detailed architectural design; do not invent them as approved facts.

## 5. Premium visual quality

The tower and its interiors must meet Hustle City's established high-fidelity standard:
- Realistic full-scale architecture and construction logic.
- Premium PBR materials: glass, steel, stone, concrete, timber, tile, polished surfaces, textiles, and metals.
- Distinct material and lighting identity for retail, casino, lounge, service, and signature hallway environments.
- High-quality close-up geometry and signage; optimized LODs and streaming at distance.
- Believable elevators, escalators, stairs, accessibility, service doors, security, staff routes, and guest circulation.
- Day/night exterior presentation and credible interior lighting.
- Original Hustle City architecture and branding. Caesar's Palace is a creator-specified fictional project destination here; do not copy the real Las Vegas property's architecture, logo, trade dress, or protected presentation.

A browser blockout is for scale, navigation, and early review only. It is not a production-ready architectural model or final visual approval.

The preview may use simplified geometry while the Unreal production assets are not yet built. Label this honestly as an early interactive blockout.
