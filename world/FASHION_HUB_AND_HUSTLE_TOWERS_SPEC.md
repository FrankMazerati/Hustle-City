# Hustle City — Diamond District: Hustle Towers Foundation

**Status:** Creator-directed foundation; detailed plans remain subject to creator review.  
**Primary engine:** Unreal Engine 5. **Browser companion:** Babylon.js preview.  
**Location — corrected:** **Diamond District**. Hustle Towers is NOT in the Fashion / Swagger District.

## 1. District separation — creator correction

The **Fashion District / Swagger District** and the **Diamond District** are separate central districts and must never be merged or treated as interchangeable.

- **Diamond District:** location of **Hustle Towers**, the 100-floor vertical landmark.
- **Fashion District / Swagger District:** a separate district with its own independent identity, geography, buildings, venues, and development plan. It is not the location of Hustle Towers.

Do not place Hustle Towers in the Fashion District in maps, browser previews, engine scenes, architecture diagrams, or future agent tasks. Exact district boundaries and map coordinates still require the creator's original map references.

## 2. Hustle Towers — creator direction

Hustle Towers is a **100-floor** landmark in the **Diamond District**. Its composition is to rise **straight up**: prioritize a vertical tower and vertical circulation rather than spreading the landmark into a sprawling horizontal campus.

The Hustle Towers development brief includes malls / shopping areas, multiple casino areas, multiple food concierge lounges, multiple welcoming help desks, the **Heavenly Hallway**, “kicking areas” (exact meaning unconfirmed), and **Caesar's Palace** (exact architectural relationship unresolved).

These are requested program elements, not finished floor assignments or completed assets.

## 3. Vertical-only massing rule

- Hustle Towers is a 100-floor vertical landmark in the Diamond District.
- The principal silhouette must be tall and vertically composed.
- Do not turn the concept into a sprawling resort campus, broad low-rise mall, or horizontally spread casino complex.
- Retail, entertainment, hospitality, food, concierge, and casino functions should be organized vertically and connected by clear internal circulation.
- Ground-level entrances and necessary service/loading access are allowed; they must not undermine the vertical-first identity.
- Any podium, connecting wing, or separate Caesar's Palace structure requires creator approval before it becomes canonical.

## 4. Program requirements — no floor allocation is locked yet

1. **Mall spaces:** one or more shopping environments, with vertical circulation and legible retail wayfinding.
2. **Casino spaces:** multiple distinct casino areas; exact number, themes, operating rules, and floor positions remain open.
3. **Food concierge lounges:** multiple lounges, with food discovery, ordering/assistance, waiting, seating, and guest support.
4. **Welcoming help desks:** multiple guest-assistance points distributed where visitors need orientation and service.
5. **Heavenly Hallway:** a named signature interior experience. Its visual style, exact location, length, and gameplay function await creator direction.
6. **Kicking areas:** preserve the requested name. Do not finalize its meaning without confirmation.
7. **Caesar's Palace:** retain the exact creator-provided name. Its architectural relationship to Hustle Towers remains unresolved.

Do not assign exact floor numbers until a floor-by-floor brief is reviewed. Keep a 100-floor register so every floor can eventually receive a unique ID, purpose, access rules, services, and content status.

## 5. Vertical circulation and building logic

The 100-floor building must be designed as a believable high-rise: multiple passenger elevator groups zoned for lower, middle, and upper floors; separated service circulation where practical; protected stair cores and emergency egress; accessible routes; structural core; mechanical/electrical/plumbing risers; plant rooms; loading/service access; and credible floor-to-floor heights.

Exact dimensions, elevator counts, structural system, floor heights, and building code assumptions must be established during detailed architectural design.

## 6. Premium visual quality

Hustle Towers and its interiors must meet Hustle City's established high-fidelity standard: realistic full-scale architecture; premium PBR glass, steel, stone, concrete, timber, tile, polished surfaces and textiles; distinct lighting/material identities; detailed close-up geometry and signage; optimized LODs; believable elevators, escalators, stairs, accessibility, security and guest circulation.

Use original Hustle City architecture and branding. Caesar's Palace is a creator-specified project destination; do not copy the real Las Vegas property's architecture, logo, trade dress, or protected presentation.

A browser blockout is for scale, navigation, and early review only. It is not a production-ready architectural model or final visual approval.

## 7. Browser-preview scope

The mobile browser companion should let the creator inspect Hustle Towers in Diamond District, switch between HIM, HCTA, Fashion / Swagger District, and Diamond District views, inspect separate district identities, and use touch-friendly controls on Android phones and tablets.

The preview may use simplified geometry while Unreal production assets are not yet built. Label it honestly as an early interactive blockout.
