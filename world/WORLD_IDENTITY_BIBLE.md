# Hustle City World Identity Bible
Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved creative direction; exact geography and district boundaries remain unresolved.

## 1. Identity
Hustle City is its own fictional place. The flagship environment takes selected inspiration from Chicago's urban character and Florida's tropical/coastal atmosphere. It is not a direct mashup, map copy, or landmark replica of Chicago and Miami. The city's shoreline, rivers, islands, skyline, street plan, district names, architecture, public spaces, signs, and landmarks must be original and internally coherent.

The first environment should feel like a major American city where dense urban life, freshwater/lakefront scale, warm coastal energy, tropical vegetation, nightlife, transit, boats, and neighborhood culture can coexist naturally.

## 2. Chicago-inspired urban vocabulary
Use as inspiration, not a tracing template:
- Dense downtown and financial core with a varied, recognizable skyline.
- Strong street grid and arterial hierarchy, alleys, bridges, elevated infrastructure and layered streetscapes.
- Brick, limestone, masonry, industrial heritage and varied neighborhood building heights.
- Elevated rail/L-train corridors and underground subway stations integrated into city blocks.
- Broad freshwater lakefront parks, beaches, promenades, piers and public gathering spaces.
- Distinct neighborhoods with their own commercial strips, residential textures, parks and civic buildings.

## 3. Florida-inspired tropical/coastal vocabulary
Use as inspiration, not a literal Miami copy:
- Warm humid air, strong sun, bright coastal haze, dramatic cloud build-up, showers and thunderstorms.
- Palm-lined boulevards and subtropical planting in suitable climate zones.
- Beaches, marinas, yacht harbors, canals, lagoons, bays, islands and waterfront homes.
- Causeways, bridges, docks, boardwalks, waterfront dining, resorts and outdoor nightlife.
- Wetlands and mangrove-inspired ecological edges where the geography supports them.
- Boats and water taxis as useful mobility options, not decorative props.

## 4. Original Hustle City visual signature
- Electric blue, white, sunset pink, purple transitions and vivid sunrise/sunset gradients are core environmental colors.
- Black and gold are selective luxury, premium and nightlife accents, not the default world palette.
- Blend daylight, sunset and nighttime lighting naturally; do not make every district look identical.
- Use reflective wet streets when rain or recent weather supports them.
- Give every district its own architecture family, planting, street furniture, signage, lighting, soundscape and activity pattern.
- Preserve the established urban fashion and luxury-streetwear culture, while allowing everyday, athletic, professional, casual and seasonal looks.

## 5. Water is a world system
Model lakes, rivers, canals, bays, lagoons, ponds, beaches, islands, wetlands, marinas and shorelines as connected geography. Define water depth/edge zones, safe access, swimming or non-swimming policy, docks, boat routes, bridges, causeways, shoreline paths, drainage and flood-prone areas. Every island needs a deliberate connection: bridge/causeway, ferry/water taxi, or another approved travel link. Do not place disconnected water features randomly.

## 6. Climate, weather and vegetation
Environment profiles must control temperature feel, humidity, precipitation, wind, cloud patterns, seasonal variation, lighting, vegetation sets and NPC activity.
- Warm coastal/tropical profile: palms, subtropical canopy, flowering plants, humid haze, heavy showers and warm nights.
- Temperate urban/lakefront profile: deciduous street trees, broad parks, lake winds, seasonal color and cooler weather.
- Wetland profile: marsh grasses, reeds, wetland trees and waterfowl habitat.
- Industrial/urban profile: hardy street planting, paved yards and weather-worn materials.
These are starting profiles, not a limit. Future U.S. regions need their own appropriate climate and scenery kits. Never scatter palms, snow, beaches or wetlands without geographic logic.

## 7. Reusable scenery archetypes
1. High-rise downtown / financial core.
2. Historic brick-and-stone mixed-use neighborhoods.
3. Elevated rail and subway corridors.
4. Lakefront parks, beaches and promenades.
5. River/canal corridors and bridge networks.
6. Tropical marina, yacht club and coastal nightlife.
7. Island districts with bridges, causeways and water transport.
8. Lagoons, wetlands and subtropical nature reserves.
9. Industrial/logistics waterfront and rail yards.
10. Suburban edges, parks, inland lakes and rural approach roads.
11. Airport and aviation corridors.
12. Future regional environment kits for other U.S. cities and landscapes.

## 8. World-building and production requirements
- Preserve the approved 7x7 master planning framework: six central district concepts and 43 potential outer neighborhood boxes; each outer box may be planned as 5x5 blocks. This is long-term capacity, not a launch commitment.
- Build in validated layers: terrain/water, macro roads/rail, districts, blocks, buildings, public realm, vegetation, props, lighting, NPC/activity and polish.
- Every scenery element must connect to a meaningful world layer: traversal, navigation, landmarks, environmental storytelling, venue/activity, or deliberate ambient decoration.
- Distinguish playable, ambient, restricted and decorative areas.
- Keep source assets, provenance and licenses traceable. Do not ship protected real-world logos, maps, landmarks or trade dress without appropriate rights.
- The world-builder foundation must generate distinct scenery profiles, not merely reskin one city.

## 9. Engine and portability
Unreal Engine 5 is the primary production runtime. Unity 6 is the secondary migration/compatibility target. Canonical environment profiles, place metadata, district rules, asset manifests and world-generation parameters remain engine-neutral in SHARED_CORE. Engine-specific terrain, foliage, water, lighting, materials, streaming and rendering implementations live in the appropriate engine adapter. Godot is retired as an active target.

The established six central district concepts remain Entertainment, Diamond, Swagger, Transportation, Hustle and Trapper. The waterfront and media campus in this section are planning clusters/areas, not newly approved district names. Do not silently add a seventh central district. Preserve the 7x7 master planning framework (six central district concepts and 43 potential outer neighborhood boxes, each outer box planned as 5x5 blocks) as long-range capacity rather than a claim that all 49 boxes must ship at launch.

**Visual-map production rule:** The first overhead plan should show the central Diamond downtown anchor, Diamond Row, the Swagger fashion district, the waterfront cluster and the blue/black media campus as recognizable, connected planning areas. It should also indicate the other established district concepts without inventing their exact boundaries. The map is a planning schematic until the creator approves precise geography. The World Architect should use it to establish visual identity, skyline massing, terrain/water, major roads and district transitions; the City Planner should later resolve exact streets, blocks, parcels, building footprints and service/transport routing.

**Unresolved:** compass orientation and exact relative placement; whether the airport shares the immediate waterfront parcel or a connected airport/waterfront zone; exact media-campus footprint; Diamond Row boundaries; lounge identities/count; exact district boundaries and street/block geometry. Do not guess these as locked canon.
