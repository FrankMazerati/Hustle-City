# Hustle City Free 99 Resource Catalog

**Policy:** $0 total spend until investor funding. No trials, subscriptions, paid upgrades, paid plugins, paid APIs, or assets whose commercial-use permission requires payment. Engine target: Unreal Engine 5 primary; Unity 6 secondary. Browser previews are a companion review surface, not the production engine.

**Catalog status:** research and acquisition manifest. Entries below are linked and screened from published source information; they have NOT been downloaded into the repository, imported into a UE project, or runtime-tested. The creator must add free assets to their own Fab/library accounts and download them on a capable workstation. Do not claim an asset is integrated until import and validation are recorded.

## Acceptance gates

1. Current download price is exactly $0 with no required contribution.
2. Commercial game use is explicitly permitted by the asset's own license/terms.
3. No paid dependency, subscription, or paid source tier is required for the intended use.
4. Editable source or importable formats are available (UE project, FBX, GLB/glTF, OBJ, or Blender source).
5. Engine version, materials, scale, topology, collisions, LODs, and performance are checked before production adoption.
6. Preserve creator name, source URL, license text/version, retrieval date, modifications, and project use. Even where attribution is not mandatory, credit creators voluntarily where practical.
7. Never redistribute a licensed asset pack as a standalone download. Keep third-party asset binaries out of public Git unless the license explicitly permits redistribution.

## Status vocabulary

- **FREE / CANDIDATE:** source page states $0 and a suitable license/use; not yet imported or technically tested.
- **FREE / BLOCKOUT ONLY:** free and useful for layout or prototyping, but style/quality is below Hustle City's final realism bar.
- **HOLD:** price, license, engine compatibility, or quality is not sufficiently established. Do not acquire or use until checked.
- **ADOPTED:** only after actual import, technical validation, and project approval. No entry is adopted yet.

## Important exclusions and cautions

- Do not use paid station environment packs, even if they look ideal. A free listing must be confirmed before any download.
- Do not treat low-poly railway assets as final visual assets. They may only support early scale, route and platform blockouts.
- Do not use the full paid versions of Quaternius or Vuena packs. Only the explicitly free Standard/sample downloads are eligible.
- Do not assume a free download means commercial rights. Record and inspect the actual license for each item.
- Do not copy real-world brands, logos, station trade dress, or protected designs into Hustle City. Use assets as editable construction references and customize the final visual identity.
- Do not place Hustle Towers in Fashion/Swagger District. It belongs in Diamond District and remains a 100-floor vertical-first landmark.
- No exact route alignments, station locations, tower floor assignments, or other unapproved geography/program decisions are introduced by this catalog.
