# Hustle City Building and Environment Framework

**Status:** Initial framework. Extend the existing [Building Identity and Interaction Catalog](../../world/BUILDING_IDENTITY_AND_INTERACTION_CATALOG.md); do not create a competing catalog.

## Purpose and visual identity
Set a consistent standard for believable, high-quality 3D buildings and public environments across the six central districts and surrounding neighborhoods. Blend Chicago-inspired urban character, Miami/Florida-inspired atmosphere and original Hustle City identity. Each district and neighborhood needs a coherent architectural language.

Prioritize credible scale, proportion, structure, materials, façade depth, windows, doors, roofs, signage, lighting, street interfaces, landscaping, utilities, weathering and age. Avoid repetitive generic blocks and low-detail placeholder-looking structures in final presentation.

## Building categories
Residential, mixed-use, retail, restaurants, nightlife, offices, civic buildings, transit facilities, industrial/service buildings, recreation, education, healthcare, parking, vacant/derelict properties and construction sites.

## Building identity record
Where relevant, specify stable building/property ID and name; type, use and occupancy; district, neighborhood, block and lot; architectural style, height/massing and façade materials; construction era and condition; floors, entrances, service access and circulation; interior access and important rooms; ownership/tenancy/business relationships as separate concepts; interactions, activities, NPC roles and schedules; visual references, lighting states and context; accessibility and believable building systems; approval status and revision history.

## Real-world plausibility
Use real-world architectural and urban references for believable proportions, construction logic, circulation and street relationships, then adapt into original fictional designs. Respect frontage, setbacks where applicable, loading/service access, pedestrian routes, transit access, neighboring structures, utilities, drainage and emergency access. Do not claim code compliance without research.

## Construction and city change
Support proposed, under-construction, partially complete, open, renovated, closed, abandoned and demolished states. Construction scenes need coherent phases, equipment, workers, safety barriers, material deliveries and temporary street/pedestrian changes.

## Production and optimization
Separate reusable modular components from unique landmarks. Track asset source, license, engine target, scale, collision, LODs, texture budgets, lighting, streaming and performance validation. Unreal Engine 5 is primary; Unity 6 is the secondary compatibility target. A concept image is not a production-ready asset.

## Quality review and open decisions
Review silhouette/scale, material quality, façade variation, entrances, interior/exterior continuity, lighting, navigation, accessibility, NPC use, district fit, construction logic and performance. District palettes, standard dimensions, modular grid, interior-access tiers, asset budgets and construction simulation depth remain to be designed and approved.
