## Operating workflow
1. Capture the original thought first, even if rough or incomplete.
2. Classify it by domain and mark its status.
3. Preserve the creator's wording and source context.
4. Refine reusable ideas into the appropriate framework.
5. Record creator-approved decisions in the decision register.
6. Update the index and link related documents.
7. Keep superseded decisions for history; never silently erase them.

## Status vocabulary
RAW IDEA · PROPOSED · APPROVED · IN DEVELOPMENT · DEFERRED · REJECTED · SUPERSEDED

## Authority and safeguards
The creator/director has final approval. Assistant and agent suggestions remain proposals until approved. Unknowns must be labeled unresolved, not invented. A framework describes intended design; it does not prove implementation. Concept images are references, not finished or licensed 3D assets. Never store credentials, secrets or private player data here.
