# Hustle City — Master Visual Blueprint & Meta AI Production Manuscript

**Status:** Creator-directed visual production brief, 2026-11-24  
**Purpose:** Single consolidated instruction set for Meta AI and the visual-reference pipeline.  
**Geographic authority:** The approved city master map, once creator-approved, is the permanent source of truth.  
**Creative authority:** The creator is final authority. Proposed colors and unplaced facilities remain proposed until approved.

## 1. Mission

Build one coherent, realistic fictional city for Hustle City, an original 3D social city-life/open-world game built primarily in Unreal Engine 5. Use supplied ChatGPT images as visual references. Do not generate unrelated city interpretations.

Images are references, not automatically production-ready 3D assets. Keep visual continuity across citywide, district, block, building, interior, character and vehicle views.

### Downtown Diamond District — black and gold
Central downtown/high-rise core. Include Diamond Row, luxury offices and businesses, upscale retail, jewelry, boutiques, hotels, fine dining, Velvet Lounge, cigar lounges, prestige destinations, premium residential buildings and polished public spaces. Distinguish luxury/prestige from Financial District's banking/corporate identity.

### Trapper District — blue and white
Gritty, distressed, lower-income neighborhood with liquor stores, convenience stores, churches, hood stores, houses, apartment buildings, trap houses, weed/dope spots as fictional environmental locations, corner businesses, weathered sidewalks, local storefronts and ordinary neighborhood life. Show residents, workers, families, young adults, older residents and business owners. Do not reduce the neighborhood to crime scenes or stereotypes.

### Hustle District — red and white
Upscale neighborhood with attractive homes, townhouses, premium residential and commercial properties, restaurants, social destinations, landscaped streets, luxury vehicles and polished public spaces. Street hustling may exist, but the district's overall clientele and appearance remain upscale.

### Swagger District — purple and silver proposed
Fashion and clothing destination: hip-hop/streetwear boutiques, clothing stores, sneaker/shoe shops, related fashion retail, social spaces, restaurants, lounges and distinctive residential/commercial architecture. Keep it distinct from Diamond District luxury boutiques and Entertainment nightlife.

### Transportation District — slate gray and teal proposed
This is an official district, not merely a citywide rail corridor. Its defining destinations are:
- Airport: terminal, arrivals/departures, runways, taxiways, aircraft parking, hangars, ground transport, passenger drop-off, parking and service roads.
- Car lot/automotive destination: vehicle dealership/marketplace, exotic and luxury vehicle display, regular-vehicle inventory, walk-through browsing, showrooms, customer entrances, circulation, parking and service/delivery access.
- Marina/yacht facilities: marina, yacht club, boat slips, docks, marine services, boat maintenance/fuel access where appropriate, passenger/service access and navigable channel to open water.

Rail, bus and citywide road systems connect through and beyond districts; do not force all transit infrastructure into this district. Keep airport runway/approach safety and shoreline/marina access physically plausible.

### Financial District — emerald and platinum/silver proposed
A distinct banking, finance and corporate-business district. Include bank headquarters, financial institutions, investment/financial services, corporate offices, business headquarters, office towers, professional services, business hotels, meeting facilities, financial plazas, upscale restaurants and commercial parking. It is not another luxury-shopping district.

## 6. Functional geography QA

A pretty image is not automatically a valid blueprint.

- Every marina/harbor must have a visible navigable opening and continuous channel to open water. Boats need sufficient depth and turning space. Breakwaters must leave an entrance; bridges need clearance or opening mechanisms. Never trap boats in an enclosed basin.
- Roads connect to roads/destinations; parking garages have entrances/exits; loading docks have service access; vehicle routes have adequate clearance.
- Airport runways connect to taxiways and aircraft parking; terminals connect to passenger and ground transport; airport roads connect to the city.
- Rail tracks connect continuously; stations sit on actual lines; elevated rail/tunnels are structurally plausible and have proper transitions.
- Buildings have usable entrances/exits, service access, logical stairs/elevators and rooftop access. Interiors must fit exterior dimensions.
- Sidewalks, crosswalks, pedestrian bridges and plazas connect to usable destinations.
- Waterways are continuous and deliberate; roads/rail respond with bridges, causeways and crossings.

Flag contradictions and propose corrections before approving an image.
