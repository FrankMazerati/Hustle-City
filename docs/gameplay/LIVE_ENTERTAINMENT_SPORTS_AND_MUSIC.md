# Hustle City Live Entertainment, Sports & Music

**Canon status:** The creator has approved the high-level direction in this document. Detailed venue rosters, event schedules, feature scope, technical implementation and commercial agreements remain to be designed.

## Core direction

Hustle City should feel like a living social destination, not only a place to complete missions. Players should be able to spend time with friends, enjoy entertainment, eat and socialize, attend events, and watch sports together.

Build the fictional entertainment foundation first. Real-world sports broadcasts and their licensing are a later phase, after the game is built and has an active player community.

**Music is the exception:** the intended experience includes real-world artists and real recorded music. Music rights must be properly secured for the intended interactive game uses; do not assume consumer streaming subscriptions grant game-use rights.

## Fictional sports first

The initial sports-viewing experience will use original fictional Hustle City content rather than real NBA, NFL or other league broadcasts.

The fictional sports system may include:
- Original basketball and football teams, leagues, matchups and seasons.
- Simulated matches presented as live broadcasts inside in-world venues.
- Original commentators, play-by-play, scores, game clocks, highlights and replays.
- Sports bars with large screens and shared watch-party experiences.
- Crowd reactions and social interactions that make watching with other players feel communal.

Exact sports, league/team identities, simulation rules, broadcast presentation, schedules and launch scope are TBD. Do not use real league branding, footage, audio or other protected material without appropriate authorization.

## Real-world sports — later phase

Real NBA, NFL and other real-world sports broadcasts are a future expansion, not a prerequisite for launch.

Consider them only after the game is built and has an active player base, and after the necessary rights and distribution agreements have been negotiated. Rights may vary by league, broadcaster, territory, platform, venue-style presentation and use. Obtain appropriate legal and licensing review before implementation.

Design the in-world screen/broadcast architecture so that a future authorized live feed could be integrated, but do not make launch dependent on it.

## Real music and artists

The creator wants real-world music by real artists in Hustle City. Music is intended to be part of the world experience, not merely an isolated menu player.

Planned experience areas include:
- Music in nightclubs, lounges, bars and other suitable venues.
- DJ sets and scheduled music events.
- Music in vehicles and in-world radio-style experiences.
- Music accompanying sports-bar events, parties and other social activities.
- Potential concerts, artist appearances and special performances.

The specific artists, songs, venues, stations, playlists, event formats and rollout are TBD.

### Music rights and release requirements

Before using a track, secure the rights needed for the specific game use. This commonly involves both the composition/publishing rights and the sound-recording/master rights; the exact rights package depends on the use and agreement.

Agreements should be reviewed for the intended platforms, territories, term, interactive/in-game playback, public or social listening features, trailers/marketing, player-created clips or streams, and any other planned exploitation. Do not assume one clearance automatically covers every use.

A personal Spotify, Apple Music or other consumer subscription is not a substitute for commercial game-use permissions. Build the music system to support approved tracks, territory/platform restrictions, metadata, cue/use reporting and replacement or takedown when required.

## Venue and social experience direction

Potential venue experiences include:
- Nightclubs and lounges with DJs, dance floors, social areas and optional VIP areas.
- Sports bars with large screens, food service, fictional sports broadcasts and watch parties.
- Concert or event venues for performances and special gatherings.

These are approved experience directions; exact venue names, locations, counts, layouts, features and launch availability remain TBD.

## Phased approach

1. Build the fictional sports-viewing and social watch-party foundation.
2. Build the real-music integration and rights-management foundation; populate it only with properly cleared music.
3. Expand DJ events, venue programming, concerts and other social entertainment.
4. Explore licensed real-world sports broadcasts once the game has an active player base and appropriate rights are secured.

## Open design and implementation work

- Final venue types, names, locations and district distribution.
- Fictional sports leagues, teams, schedules, simulation and broadcast formats.
- DJ/event scheduling, player participation and social interactions.
- Music catalogue, artist partnerships, licensing budget and rights administration.
- Radio-style experiences, playback controls, territory/platform restrictions and moderation.
- Live-stream synchronization, delivery infrastructure, performance targets and fallback behavior.
- Launch scope, accessibility, parental controls and platform-policy review where applicable.

Do not invent licensing agreements, artist participation, catalogue size, budgets or launch commitments.
