## Handoff documents reviewed

### `Hustle_City_Complete_Project_Handoff.pdf`
30-page source handoff. It establishes:
- Connected luxury-meets-street virtual city; menus should map to physical places and activities.
- Main loop: Explore → Earn → Collect → Buy → Upgrade → Travel → Socialize → Compete → Unlock → Repeat.
- Transportation/lifestyle hub clusters Hustle City International Airport, commercial flights, private jet terminal, heliport, Yacht Club and **Car Club** close together, with water, roads, hotels/resorts/casinos and skyline.
- Dashboard currencies/counters: Cash, Poker Chips, Diamonds, Wings and XP; progress categories Collections, Properties, Businesses, Cars, Travel and City Key.
- Main activity categories: Play Poker, Collect Cards, Properties, Cars, Travel and Social; bottom navigation: Missions, Events, Leaderboard and Rewards.
- Brand inventory includes Hustle City, Hustle City Gang, Mazerati Boys, OG Hustler, Our Beloved Entertainment, **Monster Beach** and Good Vibes Only.
- Modular implementation concept: account, city hub, travel, casino, properties, cars, collection, social, mission/event, economy and inventory layers.
- Explicit correction: **CAR CLUB**, never “HAR CLUB.”
- The PDF says the engine/framework was still open at the time it was written; later project decisions select Godot 4, so the newer decision log takes precedence on that specific point.

### `Hustle_City_Complete_Project_Handoff_UPDATED_Get_Wild_Beach.pdf`
30-page updated handoff. Most of the structure remains the same. It adds **Get Wild Beach** references and appears to replace the earlier **Monster Beach** wording in the beach-brand passages. Some extracted text around those passages is blank or incomplete, so treat this as a possible brand rename requiring creator confirmation—not as a fully reconciled final decision.

## Intake rules for future uploads

1. Keep original files unchanged on `main`.
2. Add a catalog entry with source filename, upload date/commit, category, what is visible, intended use, confidence and review status.
3. Distinguish visual reference from approved production asset; a screenshot does not automatically approve every UI label, icon, number or brand shown.
4. Record contradictions between documents as open questions. Do not silently merge or overwrite earlier decisions.
5. Keep environment references linked to the master world map/district plan so roads, waterways, skyline, venues and travel hubs remain geographically consistent.
6. Keep the updated color direction in the project decision log: blue/white and pink/blue are major palettes; gold/black are selective luxury accents, not the dominant palette everywhere.
7. Before production use, record asset origin, rights/license status, resolution, intended engine format, dependencies and approval state.
