# Hustle City — Flagship Master Food, Beverage & Tobacco Menu

**Status:** Creator-directed flagship catalog build. This document defines the Hustle City home-market menu only. Chicago and the other city markets are explicitly out of scope until this menu is completed and approved.

**Pricing rule:** This is the item and brand catalog, not a new game-price proposal. Do not alter existing game-price anchors or overwrite the existing draft price worksheet while building this catalog. Game prices will be assigned in a separate pass after the item list is approved. Real-world prices below are research benchmarks only; they are not game prices.

**Brand/legal note:** Real-world brands are reference examples for believable selection. Their inclusion does not grant trademark, trade-dress, music, packaging-art or product-placement rights. Confirm licensing, platform rules, age-rating treatment and regional availability before using branded assets in a released build.

## 1. Scope and menu architecture

The Hustle City menu should feel like a genuinely broad American city food, convenience-store, bar, nightclub, hotel, restaurant, cigar-lounge and liquor-store ecosystem. The same item can appear in more than one venue with different presentation and service methods.

Menu surfaces:
- Restaurants, diners, grills, takeout counters and food trucks.
- Hotels, room service, lounges, casinos, poker rooms and nightclubs.
- Bars, sports bars, rooftop venues, pool decks and bottle-service rooms.
- Convenience stores, supermarkets, liquor stores, cigar shops and tobacco counters.
- Player inventory for eligible packaged goods and persistent bottles.

Every item should eventually carry a stable item ID, display name, category, serving/package size, venue availability, inventory rules, game price, animation/service hook, age-rating flag where relevant, brand/licensing status and source/research note. All game prices remain a separate creator-approved task.

## 8. Price and free-player economy principles

Creator direction:
- Players should be able to buy ordinary food, individual shots, an occasional cigarette or cigar during a session through free gameplay earnings, without having to make a real-money purchase.
- Players should also be able to save up for an outfit or two through free play.
- Vehicles, flights, helicopters and comparable major aspirational purchases should be premium/paid-gated or otherwise require substantial progression; they should not be treated like routine session consumables.
- Preserve the existing creator price anchors and current game-price draft values as they stand. This catalog does not revise them.
- Do not make food, basic social participation, ordinary shots or occasional tobacco items a paywall.
- Model free-player income and recurring session spending before finalizing the rest of the economy. Premium purchases should support aspirational convenience/status/transport without making the ordinary social world miserable for free players.

### Spending classes to model
1. **Routine session spending:** basic meal, snack, soda/water, occasional standard shot, occasional cigar/cigarette.
2. **Lifestyle saving goals:** one or two outfits, accessories and ordinary personal items.
3. **Premium aspirational purchases:** cars, aircraft, helicopters, premium travel and other major status/lifestyle purchases.
4. **Optional premium convenience/status:** venue upgrades, premium presentation and other nonessential enhancements, subject to separate approval.

- Both sides of the transaction are recorded in the authoritative ledger.
