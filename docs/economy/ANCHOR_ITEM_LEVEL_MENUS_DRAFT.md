# Hustle City — Anchor Item-Level Menus (Draft 0.1)

**Status:** Working proposal for creator review. Nothing in this document is final menu canon until the creator approves it. Prices below are proposed in-game Hustle Money prices, not claims about current restaurant prices. Real-world menu references are used only to calibrate the price feel.

**Scope:** Build the flagship fictional Hustle City menu and the first real-city market menu, Chicago. These establish the item-level structure and shared affordability ladder for the remaining 29 markets.

## 1. Pricing rules used in this draft

- Currency: Hustle Money (displayed as $ in the draft).
- Basic meal entry point: **$5.00** (creator-provided anchor).
- Hamburger and fries: **$5.99** (creator-provided example; preserved exactly).
- Pop/soda: **$2.00–$3.00**; draft standard price **$2.50**.
- Inexpensive liquor serving: **about $5.00**; draft entry pour **$5.00**. Serving size, brands and bottle prices remain TBD.
- Other prices are proposed for review. They should be tested against expected player income, food consumption frequency, health restoration and the daily-reward model before approval.
- Menus are designed to provide affordable basics, regular meals, signature local food and premium choices. Do not make every local specialty a luxury purchase.

## 4. Menu consistency and gameplay notes

- These are proposed item records, not final game database values. IDs are draft identifiers and may change before implementation.
- All listed food and drinks use Hustle Money. Poker Chips are never accepted for food or drinks.
- The basic meal and soda anchors are intentionally consistent between the fictional home city and Chicago.
- Signature and premium items cost more because of larger portions, ingredients or specialty status—not simply because a city is different.
- A menu item should not have a health-restoration number until the health system and portion/consumption model are approved. Energy effects remain unapproved.
- The menu should allow a player to buy an inexpensive basic meal and a drink without being forced into a premium combo.
- Alcohol items are optional fictional venue inventory. Do not implement bottle sizes, real brands, alcohol effects or access rules from this draft alone.
- Prices must be tested against expected free-player income and ordinary play-session consumption before being accepted.

## 6. Creator review required

Please review these decisions before this becomes approved menu canon:

1. Whether the proposed item selection captures the flagship Hustle City menu and Chicago's local identity.
2. Whether the proposed prices feel right relative to the creator's anchors.
3. Whether the menu should include additional categories or specific signature dishes.
4. Whether any proposed item should be removed, renamed or reserved for a specific venue.

**Not approved by this draft:** final item prices, portion sizes, food effects, energy effects, alcohol serving volumes, bottle sizes/prices, venue-specific availability, taxes/tips or final daily rewards.
